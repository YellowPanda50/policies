This policy explains how Simple Signpad handles information. Yellow Panda provides the app. You can reach us at yellowpanda50@gmail.com.

Your signature

The signature you draw stays on your device. When you tap Save, a JPEG image is stored in Pictures/SignaturePad and an SVG file is stored in Download/SignaturePad. There is no account, and we do not upload your signature to a server of our own.

Ads

The only advertising provider in this app is Google AdMob. An anchored banner may appear at the bottom of the sign screen, below the area where you draw. The banner does not cover the signature. The About screen may show a native ad. A full-screen ad may appear when you leave About and return to the sign screen. It is not shown when the app opens, when you exit, while you are signing, or when you save.

When an ad is requested, Google may process the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device information (such as model, language, and screen size), and how you interact with the ad (such as views and taps). Google describes that processing at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

We do not sell your signature, and we do not send the signature image to AdMob.

The app uses the internet so ads can load. It does not request your location, contacts, or microphone. On older Android versions it may ask for storage permission so a saved signature can be written to your device.

What we do not do

We do not sell your signature. We do not ask you to create an account. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Email, share, Facebook, and rating

If you email us, share the app, open our Facebook page, or rate the app, the app you choose (for example your mail app or Google Play) handles that step. We receive only what you send us yourself, such as an email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text is shown in the app.
