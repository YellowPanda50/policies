Privacy Policy

Yellow Panda
Effective date: 26 September 2026

This policy explains how Property Loan Calculator handles information. Yellow Panda provides the app. You can reach us at yellowpanda50@gmail.com.

Information you enter

Loan amounts, interest rates, years, property prices, rent, income, and other numbers you type are used only to calculate results on your device. There is no account, and we do not upload those figures to a server of our own.

Ads policy

The only ad provider in this app is Google AdMob. Ads may appear as a banner on the home screen and the calculators, as a native ad on About, and as a full-screen ad when you move from one section to another. A full-screen ad is shown at most once every two section changes. It is not shown when the app opens, when you exit, or on top of a result you just calculated.

When an ad is requested, Google may process the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device information (such as model, language, and screen size), and how you interact with the ad (such as views and taps). Google may use this to show personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure and prevent invalid traffic. Google describes that processing at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

We do not sell the numbers you enter to advertisers, and we do not pass those figures to AdMob.

Where the law requires a choice, the app asks for consent before requesting ads, using Google’s consent message. You can allow personalized ads, allow only non-personalized ads, or refuse ads where that option is offered. If Privacy options appears on the About screen, you can change that choice there. You can also control Google ads on your device at https://adssettings.google.com.

Permissions

The app uses the internet and network status so ads can load. It does not request your location, contacts, photos, or microphone.

What we do not do

We do not sell the numbers you enter. We do not ask you to create an account. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Email, share, Facebook, and rating

If you email us, share the app, open our Facebook page, or rate the app, the app you pick (for example your mail app or Google Play) handles that step. We receive only what you send us yourself, such as an email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be shown in the app.
