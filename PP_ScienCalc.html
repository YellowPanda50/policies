This policy explains how Scientific Calculator handles information. Yellow Panda provides the app. You can reach us at yellowpanda50@gmail.com.

Information you enter

Expressions and results are calculated on your device. There is no account, and we do not upload those figures to a server of our own.

Ads policy

The only ad provider in this app is Google AdMob. A banner may appear at the bottom of the calculator. The About screen may show a native ad. A full-screen ad may appear when you leave About and return to the calculator. It is not shown when the app opens, when you exit, or while you are calculating.

When an ad is requested, Google may process the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device information (such as model, language, and screen size), and how you interact with the ad (such as views and taps). Google describes that processing at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

We do not sell the numbers you enter, and we do not pass those figures to AdMob.

The app uses the internet so ads can load. It does not request your location, contacts, photos, or microphone.

What we do not do

We do not sell the numbers you enter. We do not ask you to create an account. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Email, share, Facebook, and rating

If you email us, share the app, open our Facebook page, or rate the app, the app you pick (for example your mail app or Google Play) handles that step. We receive only what you send us yourself, such as an email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be shown in the app.
