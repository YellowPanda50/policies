This policy explains how Currency Exchange and Converter handles information. Yellow Panda provides the app. You can reach us at yellowpanda50@gmail.com.

Exchange rates

The app downloads public exchange rates from Frankfurter (https://www.frankfurter.app), which publishes European Central Bank reference rates. The request includes the currency you ask for. We do not send your name, and we do not run our own rate server. The latest rates, the rate date, and the pairs you mark as favorites are saved only on your phone so the converter still works offline. Uninstalling the app removes that data from the device.

Ads policy

The only ad provider in this app is Google AdMob. Ads may appear as a banner on Convert, Exchange rates, and Favorites, as a native ad on About, and as a full-screen ad when you leave About for another section.

When an ad is requested, Google may process the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device information (such as model, language, and screen size), and how you interact with the ad (such as views and taps). Google may use this to show personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure and prevent invalid traffic. Google describes that processing at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

We do not sell your saved pairs to advertisers, and we do not pass amounts or currency pairs to AdMob.

Where the law requires a choice, the app asks for consent before requesting ads, using Google's consent message. You can allow personalized ads, allow only non-personalized ads, or refuse ads where that option is offered. If Privacy options appears on the About screen, you can change that choice there. You can also control Google ads on your device at https://adssettings.google.com.

Permissions

The app uses the internet and network status so rates and ads can load. It does not request your location, contacts, photos, or microphone.

What we do not do

We do not sell your saved pairs. We do not ask you to create an account. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Email, share, Facebook, and rating

If you email us, share the app, open our Facebook page, or rate the app, the app you pick (for example your mail app or Google Play) handles that step. We receive only what you send us yourself, such as an email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be shown in the app.
